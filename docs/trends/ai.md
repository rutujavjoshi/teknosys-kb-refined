# AI in Documentation

AI is changing how documentation is created—but not *what good documentation looks like*.

It can assist writing, but it cannot replace structured thinking.

---

## Why this matters

- Speeds up content creation  
- Helps with drafts and summaries  
- Supports consistency  
- Reduces repetitive work  

---

## Where AI fits in the workflow


Research → Draft → Refine → Review → Publish
↑
AI assists here


---

## Practical use cases

### 1. Drafting content

AI can help:
- Generate first drafts  
- Expand outlines  
- Suggest examples  

---

### 2. Editing and refinement

AI can:
- Improve clarity  
- Simplify language  
- Check grammar  

---

### 3. Content transformation

AI helps convert:
- Long content → summaries  
- Text → structured steps  
- Notes → documentation  

---

## What AI cannot do well

- Understand user intent deeply  
- Replace domain knowledge  
- Ensure accuracy without validation  
- Design content structure  

---

## Common mistakes

- Copy-pasting AI output without review  
- Over-relying on AI for structure  
- Ignoring accuracy checks  

---

## Practical guidance

- Use AI for drafts, not final content  
- Always review for accuracy and clarity  
- Combine AI output with your expertise  
- Treat AI as a tool, not a replacement
