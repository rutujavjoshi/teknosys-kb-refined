# Workplace Communication

Technical writing is not just about writing.

It’s about working with:
- Developers  
- Product managers  
- Designers  
- Stakeholders  

---

## Why this matters

- Improves content quality  
- Reduces rework  
- Builds credibility  
- Enables smoother workflows  

---

## The communication flow

In most teams:


Writer ↔ Developer ↔ Product Manager ↔ Stakeholders


---

## Key communication scenarios

### 1. Working with developers

- Clarify technical details  
- Ask precise questions  
- Validate assumptions  

---

### 2. Working with product managers

- Understand feature intent  
- Align documentation scope  
- Track changes  

---

### 3. Handling feedback

- Separate useful feedback from noise  
- Ask for clarification when needed  
- Avoid defensive responses  

---

## Common challenges

- Incomplete information  
- Conflicting inputs  
- Last-minute changes  
- Lack of documentation ownership  

---

## Practical guidance

- Ask questions early, not during review  
- Document decisions (not just content)  
- Communicate clearly and concisely  
- Build relationships with SMEs
