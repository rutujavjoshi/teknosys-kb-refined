# Career Growth & Portfolio

Technical writing is not a static role.

It evolves with:
- Tools  
- Technologies  
- Industry expectations  

---

## Why this matters

- Helps you stay relevant  
- Expands career opportunities  
- Builds professional credibility  

---

## Career growth areas


Writing → Tools → Domain → Strategy


---

## 1. Writing skills

- Clarity and structure  
- Audience awareness  
- Content design  

---

## 2. Tools and workflows

- Docs-as-Code  
- Authoring tools  
- Version control  

---

## 3. Domain knowledge

- APIs  
- Systems  
- Industry-specific knowledge  

---

## 4. Strategic skills

- Information architecture  
- Content strategy  
- Analytics  

---

## Building a portfolio

A strong portfolio should include:

- Writing samples  
- Documentation projects  
- Tools used  
- Problem-solving examples  

---

## Common mistakes

- Only showcasing final output  
- Ignoring process and decisions  
- Not updating portfolio regularly  

---

## Practical guidance

- Document your work, not just results  
- Show before-and-after improvements  
- Include real-world scenarios  
- Keep your portfolio updated
